MoHRE Emiratization Compliance — Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) & Rules
(Government of the UAE — Ministry of Human Resources & Emiratisation. Demo policy.)

PURPOSE
This SOP defines how business (G2B) Emiratization reports are requested, reviewed
and followed up. The assistant reviews submissions and recommends actions only;
a MoHRE officer makes every final legal or enforcement decision.

1. REPORTING REQUIREMENT
1.1 Every registered private-sector establishment with 20 or more employees must
    submit an Emiratization report each quarter.
1.2 The reporting deadline is the 15th day of the month following each quarter.
1.3 A 10-working-day grace period applies before a case is treated as overdue.

2. WHAT A COMPLETE REPORT MUST CONTAIN
A report is COMPLETE only if it includes ALL of the following:
2.1 Total number of employees.
2.2 Number of UAE nationals employed.
2.3 Emiratization percentage (UAE nationals ÷ total employees × 100).
2.4 New UAE-national hires and any UAE-national departures during the quarter.
2.5 A signed declaration from an authorised company representative.
2.6 Supporting evidence for UAE-national hires (offer letters or WPS records).
If any of 2.1–2.6 is missing, mark the case "Incomplete Submission" and request the
specific missing item(s).

3. EMIRATIZATION TARGETS
3.1 Establishments with 50+ skilled employees must maintain at least 2% UAE nationals
    in skilled roles, rising 1% per year toward the national target.
3.2 A firm below its required percentage is NOT automatically penalised — flag it
    "Escalation Required" and refer it to an officer with a short summary.

4. REVIEW OUTCOMES (allowed statuses)
Use only these statuses: Pending Outreach, Report Requested, Response Received,
Incomplete Submission, Under Review, Follow-up Sent, Escalation Required, Completed.
4.1 On-time, complete, and meeting target  → "Completed".
4.2 Complete but below target              → "Escalation Required".
4.3 Missing any required item              → "Incomplete Submission".
4.4 No reply within the deadline           → "Follow-up Sent" (first reminder),
                                              then "Escalation Required" after 2 reminders.

5. FOLLOW-UP & ESCALATION
5.1 Send a first polite reminder on the deadline; a second after 5 working days.
5.2 After two unanswered reminders, or for any below-target or sensitive case,
    set "Escalation Required" and prepare a one-paragraph summary for the officer.
5.3 Never threaten fines or legal action in any message — that is the officer's decision.

6. COMMUNICATION TONE
All business emails must use a professional, respectful MoHRE tone, be concise,
reference the specific report/quarter, and state a clear next step and due date.
Provide Arabic alongside English where appropriate.

7. WHAT THE ASSISTANT MUST NOT DO
It must not make final legal or enforcement decisions, must not impose penalties,
and must not close a case as compliant without an officer's confirmation for any
below-target or escalated case. It reviews, identifies gaps, recommends next actions,
updates the tracking record, and escalates when needed.
